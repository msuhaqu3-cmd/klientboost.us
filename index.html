Questions agents ask us.

Still unsure? Book a quick call and we'll check availability in your area.

What kind of leads do I get?+

Geotargeted buyers and sellers in your selected zip codes who are actively looking to make a move.

Are my referrals exclusive?+

Yes. Referrals in your selected areas go to you, so you're not competing with other agents in our network for the same lead.

How are prospects verified?+

Our team contacts each prospect to confirm their details, property preferences, budget and timeline before the referral reaches you.

When do I pay the referral fee?+

Only at closing: 15% on Flex, Essential and Advanced, and 10% on Elite. If a referral doesn't close, there's no fee on it.

Should I choose monthly or annual?+

Flex is $200, billed monthly. Annual plans cost much less over a year, from $375, and give you more referrals and zip codes as you move up to Advanced and Elite.

What if a referral doesn't work out?+

Flex and Elite include referral replacement.
