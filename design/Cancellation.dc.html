1. Term and cancellation

The referral service agreement lasts one year from its effective date. Either KlientBoost or the agent/broker may end it early, with or without cause, by giving written or emailed notice at least 30 days before the intended termination date.

2. Effect of termination

Ending the agreement does not cancel amounts already owed or earned, including referral fees, credits or discounts from referrals accepted before termination.

3. Cancelling your membership

Your membership runs from your signup date until the agreement ends. To cancel, give at least 30 days' written or emailed notice and complete our service cancellation form. The membership fee is non-refundable after the signup date.

4. How to cancel

  1. Request the service cancellation form from our team.
  2. Complete the form.
  3. Email it to support@klientboost.us at least 30 days before your intended cancellation date.
